## Buying Guide: What to Look For

Choosing the right ultrasonic cleaner involves more than just picking the largest tank. Consider these three factors:

* **Frequency (kHz):** Most automotive cleaners operate around 40kHz. This is the "sweet spot" for removing heavy grease and carbon deposits without causing cavitation erosion on soft aluminum engine parts.
* **Tank Size vs. Part Size:** Always measure your largest typical part (like a cylinder head or carburetor) and add 20% to account for the basket and cleaning solution displacement.
* **Heating Element:** Cold liquid does not clean grease. Ensure your unit features an adjustable heater capable of reaching at least 60°C (140°F), as most commercial-grade cleaning detergents work best at these temperatures.
* **Durability:** Look for "Industrial Grade" specifically. Cheap residential units will burn out their transducers within a few weeks of constant daily use in a shop environment.
