## Buying Guide: What to Look For in 2025

Choosing the right CRM is a long-term business decision. Here is what you must demand from your software provider:

*   **Dispatch Optimization:** Does the system use AI to assign the nearest, most qualified tech? At 20+ techs, manual dispatching is losing you money every day.
*   **Preventive Maintenance (PM) Management:** Your commercial contracts are your bread and butter. Ensure the software can automate PM scheduling, trigger recurring invoices, and provide a clear dashboard of upcoming service cycles.
*   **Integration Ecosystem:** Does it sync with your current accounting software (QuickBooks, Sage, Xero)? Data silos are the enemy of growth.
*   **Customizable Reporting:** As an enterprise owner, you need to see profit per client, tech efficiency, and call-back rates at a glance.

## Conclusion

If your HVAC business has hit the scale where manual processes are choking your profitability, it is time to transition to a purpose-built CRM.
