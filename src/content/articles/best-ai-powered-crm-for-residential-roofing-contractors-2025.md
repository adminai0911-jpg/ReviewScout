## Buying Guide: How to Choose the Right AI-Powered CRM

When investing in technology for your roofing business, don't just look at the price tag. Look at the ROI. Here is what you should consider:

*   **AI Lead Prioritization:** Does the CRM use AI to analyze lead behavior and tell your sales reps who to call first? This is a non-negotiable in 2025.
*   **Mobile Functionality:** Your team is on roofs, not in chairs. Ensure the CRM has a rock-solid mobile app that allows for on-site photos, digital signatures, and real-time updates.
*   **Integration Ecosystem:** Does it sync with your current accounting software (like QuickBooks) and your supplier portals? If it doesn't "talk" to your other tools, you are creating more manual work.
*   **Customer Support:** Roofing doesn't stop at 5 PM on a Friday. Ensure the provider offers reliable, industry-specific support.
