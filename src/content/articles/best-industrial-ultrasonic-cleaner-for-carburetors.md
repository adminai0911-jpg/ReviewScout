## Buying Guide: What to Look For

Selecting the right industrial cleaner isn't just about the biggest tank. Consider these three factors:

*   **Capacity:** Always measure your largest carburetor assembly. If you restore four-cylinder motorcycles, do not buy anything smaller than a 20L tank, or you will be forced to split the banks, which creates more work for you.
*   **Heating Element:** A heated tank is non-negotiable. Carburetor cleaner solutions work significantly faster and more effectively when the liquid is maintained between 50°C and 60°C.
*   **Frequency:** Most industrial units operate at 40kHz, which is the "sweet spot" for automotive parts. This frequency is powerful enough to remove heavy grease without being so aggressive that it damages soft aluminum or brass components.
