## Buying Guide for Medical Device CRM Software

When shopping for an enterprise CRM in this niche, avoid looking at "Sales Features" alone. Focus on these three pillars:

### 1. Regulatory Compliance (HIPAA/HITECH)
Does the CRM provide a clear audit trail of every interaction? In the medical device world, if an interaction isn't documented according to industry standards, it didn't happen. Ensure your vendor offers a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).

### 2. Procurement Mapping
Your sales cycle likely involves Value Analysis Committees (VACs), surgeons, and supply chain managers. Your CRM must allow you to map these influencers into a single account, tracking who is the champion, who is the detractor, and what documentation they need to sign off on a purchase.

### 3. Field Mobility
Medical device sales reps spend 80% of their time outside the office. The mobile application of your CRM must be robust—allowing for photo uploads (for inventory tracking), voice-to-text logging for call reports, and offline access in hospitals with poor connectivity.
