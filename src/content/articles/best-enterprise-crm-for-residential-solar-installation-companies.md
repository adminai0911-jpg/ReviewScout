## Buying Guide: How to Choose the Right Solar CRM

When selecting an enterprise CRM for your solar business, don't just look for "leads management." You need a platform that understands the specific lifecycle of a solar installation:

1. **Integrated Proposal Generation:** Can the CRM pull data from your design tools? You shouldn't have to re-enter customer data twice. 
2. **Permitting & Utility Workflow:** Look for automated status tracking. Can the system automatically notify customers when a permit is approved or a utility inspection is scheduled?
3. **Inventory Management:** Your CRM must be able to link sold systems to physical inventory in your warehouse. If the software doesn't know you’re out of microinverters, you're headed for a project stall.
4. **API and Integrations:** Your CRM is the heart of your business. Ensure it plays well with your accounting software (like QuickBooks) and your marketing automation platforms.
