## Buying Guide: What to Look For

Choosing the right cleaner comes down to three primary factors:

1.  **Capacity:** Measure your typical batch size. A 6-liter unit is perfect for most club shooters, but if you process brass in bulk for a full season, go for a 9-liter unit like the [Hornady Lock-N-Load Hot Tub](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Hornady+Lock-N-Load+Hot+Tub&tag=reviewscout-20).
2.  **Heating Element:** Never buy a cold-water ultrasonic cleaner for brass. Heat is essential for liquefying stubborn carbon buildup. Ensure the unit has an adjustable thermostat.
3.  **Transducer Quality:** The transducer is the "heart" of the cleaner. Industrial-grade units utilize higher-quality transducers that provide consistent, long-term cleaning power without burning out.
4.  **Timer Function:** Look for a digital interface rather than a mechanical knob. You want repeatable results, and digital timers allow you to time your cycles perfectly for consistency.
