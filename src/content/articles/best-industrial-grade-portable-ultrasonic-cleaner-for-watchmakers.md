## Buying Guide: What Watchmakers Must Consider

When selecting an ultrasonic cleaner for watchmaking in 2025, look beyond the price tag. Here is what separates a generic cleaner from an industrial watchmaker’s tool:

* **Frequency:** Standard ultrasonic cleaners operate at 40kHz. While fine for general cleaning, some professional watchmakers prefer higher frequencies (80kHz+) to prevent surface damage to highly polished movement plates.
* **Degassing Function:** A "degas" cycle is non-negotiable. It removes dissolved gases from the cleaning solution, allowing the ultrasonic waves to work at maximum efficiency.
* **Heating Capabilities:** Heated cleaning significantly speeds up the breakdown of old oils (epilame and synthetic lubricants). Ensure the unit has a thermostat that is accurate within 1-2 degrees.
* **Tank Material:** Always opt for 316-grade stainless steel, which is resistant to the corrosive nature of specialized watch cleaning fluids like L&R or Zenith.
