## Buying Guide: Selecting the Right ERP for Food Manufacturing

When investing in a long-term enterprise solution, ignore the marketing fluff and focus on these critical operational pillars:

### 1. Traceability and Recall Management
In the food industry, a recall can be a company-ending event. Your ERP must provide "one-click" backward and forward traceability. Ensure the software can link batch numbers to specific suppliers and customers instantly.

### 2. Recipe and Formula Management
Food production is not standard manufacturing. You need a system that handles "bill of materials" as recipes. This includes managing percentages, batch sizing, scaling for different packaging, and the ability to track nutritional labeling automatically.

### 3. Catch Weight and Inventory Aging
If you sell by the unit but purchase by weight (or vice versa), ensure your ERP handles catch weight management natively. Furthermore, software that automatically alerts you to "First Expired, First Out" (FEFO) inventory management is essential for minimizing waste.

### 4. Regulatory Compliance (FSMA/GFSI)
Your ERP should automate the collection of data required by the FDA. If the system simplifies the documentation for audits, it will save your quality assurance team hundreds of hours per year.
