## Buying Guide: Choosing the Right Enterprise CRM

When selecting software for your commercial HVAC operations, consider these three pillars:

### 1. Dispatch Scalability
Commercial work is rarely "first-come, first-served." You need a CRM that allows you to prioritize high-value SLAs and handle emergency equipment failures without disrupting your scheduled preventative maintenance routes. Look for drag-and-drop interfaces that provide real-time visibility into tech location and skill sets.

### 2. Deep CRM Integration
Do not settle for a tool that holds customer data in a silo. Your CRM should pull data into your accounting software, field mobile app, and marketing automation platform. If the sales team can't see the service history, they can't sell the right maintenance plan.

### 3. Reporting & Analytics
You can’t improve what you don’t measure. Your CRM must provide automated dashboards showing your "True Cost" per service call, technician utilization rates, and the profitability of your long-term commercial contracts.
