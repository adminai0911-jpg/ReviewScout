## Buying Guide: Choosing the Right CRM for Your HVAC Business

When evaluating an enterprise CRM, do not let "shiny object syndrome" dictate your choice. Focus on these three pillars:

### 1. Integration Capability
Your CRM must play nice with your accounting software. If your field technicians are using an app that doesn’t sync perfectly with your invoicing system, you are losing money on administrative overhead. 

### 2. Preventative Maintenance (PM) Automation
Commercial HVAC is built on PM contracts. Ensure the software can automate recurring service reminders, generate work orders without manual intervention, and track equipment lifespan across multiple customer sites.

### 3. Mobile Performance
Your field technicians are the face of your company. If the mobile app crashes or is too complex to navigate, they will bypass it. Test the mobile app on older hardware during the demo phase; if it’s slow on an old tablet, it will be a nightmare in the field.
