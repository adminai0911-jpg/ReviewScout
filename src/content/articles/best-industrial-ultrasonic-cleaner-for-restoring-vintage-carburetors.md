There is nothing more frustrating for a restoration enthusiast than a vintage carburetor that refuses to idle correctly due to decades-old, rock-hard varnish hiding in the internal circuits. ## Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

When shopping for an ultrasonic cleaner for vintage carburetors, keep these three factors in mind:

1. **Tank Size:** Measure your largest carburetor body before purchasing. Ensure the tank dimensions allow for the part to be fully submerged without touching the bottom (where the transducers are located).
2. **Heating Capability:** Carburetor varnish is organic and resistant. A heater that can reach 60°C (140°F) is non-negotiable for effective cleaning.
3. **Frequency:** Industrial cleaners typically operate between 35kHz and 45kHz. This range is the "sweet spot" for automotive parts, as it provides strong scrubbing power without being so high that it erodes the aluminum surface of the carburetor.
4. **Degas Function:** Look for a "degas" feature if you want to be truly professional. This removes air from the cleaning solution, making the cavitation process significantly more efficient.
