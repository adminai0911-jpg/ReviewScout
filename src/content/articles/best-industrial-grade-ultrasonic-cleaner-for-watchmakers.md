## Buying Guide: What Professional Watchmakers Need to Know

When selecting an ultrasonic cleaner for horology, look for these key technical features:

*   **Frequency (kHz):** Most professional watch cleaning requires 40kHz. Lower frequencies can be too aggressive, potentially marring soft metals, while higher frequencies may not provide the mechanical scrubbing necessary for thick, old oils.
*   **Sweep Mode:** This ensures the sound waves move within the tank. Without sweep mode, dead zones can occur, leading to uneven cleaning—the last thing you want when dealing with a high-grade movement.
*   **Degassing:** Fresh cleaning solution contains dissolved gases that impede cavitation. A "Degas" function pre-treats the solution, making the subsequent cleaning cycle significantly more efficient.
*   **Indirect Cleaning Method:** Never put watch parts directly into the main tank. Always use beakers or glass jars suspended in the water bath. This protects the tank and allows you to use specialized solvents without contaminating the entire unit.
