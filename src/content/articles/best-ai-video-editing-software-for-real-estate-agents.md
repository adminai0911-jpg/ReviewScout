## Buying Guide for Real Estate Agents

When selecting your AI video software, consider these three pillars:

1.  **Automation vs. Control:** Do you need a tool that does everything for you ([CapCut](https://automesion.com/?ref=reviewscout)), or do you need granular control over the look and feel of the video ([Adobe Premiere Pro](https://automesion.com/?ref=reviewscout))? 
2.  **Workflow Integration:** Can the software easily pull files from your cloud storage or directly from your phone? The best software is the one you will actually use every week.
3.  **AI Capabilities:** Look for "generative" features. Being able to remove objects from a scene or automatically generate captions is now the standard for high-converting real estate video.

## Conclusion

The "Best" software is ultimately the one that reduces your time-to-market.
