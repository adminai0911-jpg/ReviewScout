## Buying Guide: What to Look For

When shopping for an ultrasonic cleaner for carburetors, keep these three factors in mind:

* **Tank Size:** Don’t just look at the liters; look at the dimensions. A long, narrow tank is often better for a row of motorcycle carbs than a deep, square tank. Ensure your largest carburetor body fits comfortably with at least an inch of clearance on all sides.
* **Heating Function:** Cleaning fuel varnish is significantly faster and more effective with heat. Always choose a model with a built-in heater. You generally want to run your solution between 120°F and 140°F for optimal carburetor cleaning.
* **Frequency (kHz):** Most industrial units operate around 40kHz, which is the industry standard for cleaning engine parts. It’s strong enough to remove grease and carbon without damaging delicate aluminum castings.
* **The Solution Matters:** Never use gasoline or flammable solvents in an ultrasonic cleaner. Always use a dedicated, water-based biodegradable ultrasonic carburetor cleaning solution.
