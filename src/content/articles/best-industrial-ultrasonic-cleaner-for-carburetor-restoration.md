## Buying Guide: What to Look For

When selecting an ultrasonic cleaner for carburetors, don't be swayed solely by price or "cool factor." Focus on these three metrics:

* **Tank Capacity:** Measure the widest dimension of the carburetor banks you plan to clean. Always size up; if you force a rack of carbs into a tank, the cleaning will be uneven, and you risk damaging the unit’s side walls.
* **Heating Element:** Never buy a professional cleaner without a heater. Warm solution (usually between 50°C and 60°C) is essential to breaking down polymerized fuel (varnish).
* **Transducer Quality:** This is the heart of the machine. Industrial-grade transducers provide a uniform distribution of ultrasonic energy. Avoid units with a single small transducer if you are cleaning large, heavy metal parts, as they will leave "dead zones" in the tank.
* **Cleaning Chemistry:** Your results are only as good as your solution. Ensure you pair these machines with specialized carburetor cleaning concentrates rather than dish soap or harsh acids that can pit the aluminum surfaces of your carburetors.
