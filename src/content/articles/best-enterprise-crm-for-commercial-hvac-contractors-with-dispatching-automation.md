## Buying Guide: What to Look For in 2025

When selecting a CRM and dispatching tool for your commercial HVAC business, don't just look for "features." Look for these three pillars of enterprise growth:

### 1. Skill-Based Dispatching
In commercial work, you cannot send a residential tech to a complex chiller repair. Ensure your software supports **skill-based routing**, where the system automatically matches the work order requirements to the certification level of your technician.

### 2. Preventative Maintenance (PM) Contract Automation
The most profitable HVAC companies live on recurring revenue. Your software must automatically generate recurring work orders, email them to the client, and prompt for scheduling without human intervention. 

### 3. API & Integration Depth
Your CRM should not be an island. It must talk to your accounting software (like QuickBooks or Sage), your supplier's inventory systems, and potentially your building automation system (BAS) alerts to trigger automated dispatching when equipment throws an error code.
