## Buying Guide: What to Look for in an Ergonomic Chair Under $300

Choosing the right ergonomic chair, especially when adhering to a budget, requires careful consideration. Here’s what software engineers should prioritize:

*   **Adjustability is King:** Look for chairs with multiple adjustment points. This includes seat height, seat depth (especially important for taller or shorter individuals), armrest height and pivot, backrest tilt with tension control, and ideally, adjustable lumbar support. The more customizable, the better you can tailor the chair to your unique body.
*   **Lumbar Support:** Non-negotiable for engineers. Ensure the chair offers adequate and preferably adjustable lumbar support to maintain the natural curve of your lower spine.
*   **Breathable Materials:** Mesh backs are excellent for airflow, preventing sweat build-up during long coding sessions. If you prefer fabric, look for breathable options.
*   **Seat Comfort:** A waterfall seat edge helps reduce pressure on your thighs, improving circulation. High-density foam is crucial for long-term comfort and durability.
*   **Build Quality & Durability:** Even under $300, you can find chairs with sturdy frames (steel or heavy-duty nylon) and reliable components. Check reviews for comments on longevity. A good warranty is a bonus.
*   **Weight Capacity:** Ensure the chair can comfortably support your weight and height.
*   **Assembly:** While not an ergonomic feature, easy assembly saves time and frustration. Look for chairs with clear instructions and minimal parts.
