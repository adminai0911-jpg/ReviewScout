## Buying Guide: What Professional Watchmakers Need to Know

When selecting a cleaner for horological use, do not be fooled by "jewelry cleaners." A true professional cleaner must have:

* **Frequency:** Always look for 40kHz. Lower frequencies are too aggressive and can damage delicate escapement parts, while higher frequencies may not provide the "scrubbing" power needed for dried synthetic oils.
* **Degas Function:** This is non-negotiable. Degassing removes trapped air from your cleaning solution, which is essential for ensuring the cavitation bubbles collapse with maximum force on the movement parts.
* **Basket Quality:** Since you are dealing with balance staffs and tiny movement screws, the included basket must have a fine enough mesh to prevent loss while allowing maximum fluid flow.
* **Chemical Compatibility:** Ensure your unit is compatible with the solvents you plan to use. Some cheaper plastics inside the tank or lid will melt when exposed to the potent chemicals required for deep-cleaning watch movements.
