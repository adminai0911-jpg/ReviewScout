## Buying Guide: What to Look For

When choosing an AI-powered CRM for your roofing business, keep these three criteria in mind:

### 1. Automation Capabilities
Look for a CRM that offers "If-This-Then-That" workflows. If a homeowner requests an estimate on your website, the CRM should automatically send an email, create a contact profile, and remind you to follow up within 24 hours.

### 2. Aerial Measurement Integration
Roofing is visual. Ensure your CRM integrates natively with aerial measurement tools (like EagleView or Hover). Manually inputting measurements is a time-sink that AI should be solving for you.

### 3. Mobile Accessibility
Your crew is on the roof, not in an office. If the mobile app is slow or confusing, your team won't use it. Test the mobile version before committing; your CRM is only as good as the data entered into it by your field team.
