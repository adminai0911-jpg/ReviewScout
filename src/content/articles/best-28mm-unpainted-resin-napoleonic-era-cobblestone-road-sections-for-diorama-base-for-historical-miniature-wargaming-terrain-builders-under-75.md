### Buying Guide: Choosing Your Perfect 28mm Napoleonic Cobblestone Roads

When investing in **28mm unpainted resin Napoleonic era cobblestone road sections for your diorama base**, especially when aiming for under $75, consider these crucial factors to ensure you make the best choice for your Historical miniature wargaming terrain:

*   **Scale Accuracy (28mm):** Ensure the cobblestone size and road width are appropriate for your 28mm miniatures. Oversized or undersized stones can look jarring and break immersion. All our recommendations are designed with 28mm in mind.
*   **Resin Quality & Durability:** Good quality resin terrain is durable, resists warping, and holds fine detail well. Look for pieces with minimal flashing and no air bubbles. Durable pieces will withstand frequent use on your gaming table.
*   **Napoleonic Era Authenticity:** Examine the style of the cobblestones. Are they irregularly shaped, as was common in that period, or too uniform? Authentic detailing enhances the historical feel of your diorama.
*   **Modularity & Layout Flexibility:** How many different types of sections are included (straights, curves, intersections, T-junctions)? More variety means greater flexibility in creating dynamic and unique road layouts for your battlefield.
*   **Ease of Painting (Unpainted Resin):** Since you're buying unpainted, consider how well the texture will take paint. Crisp, clean details allow for easier drybrushing and washing, bringing out the depth of the cobblestones with less effort.
*   **Value for Money (Under $75):** While quality is paramount, staying within budget is key. Evaluate the number of sections provided for the price. Sometimes a slightly higher price for a more comprehensive or detailed set can offer better long-term value.
