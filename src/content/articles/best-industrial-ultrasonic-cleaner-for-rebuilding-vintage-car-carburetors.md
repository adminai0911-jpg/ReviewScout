## Buying Guide: What to Look For

When shopping for an ultrasonic cleaner for carburetors, consider these three critical factors:

* **Tank Capacity:** Measure the widest part of your carburetor. You don't want to buy a tank where you have to flip the carb halfway through cleaning. A 10L unit is generally the minimum for V8 carbs.
* **Heating Element:** Cold cleaning solution won't effectively remove years of baked-on carbon. Ensure the unit has a built-in heater (ideally adjustable up to 80°C).
* **Transducer Quality:** This is the heart of the machine. Industrial-grade transducers last longer and produce more consistent cavitation. Avoid units that feel too "lightweight," as they often use inferior components that fail under the heat required for carb cleaning.
* **Cleaning Solution:** Remember that these cleaners are only as good as the solution you put in them. Use a specialized ultrasonic carburetor cleaner fluid, as dish soap or improper solvents can corrode delicate aluminum alloys common in vintage carburetors.
