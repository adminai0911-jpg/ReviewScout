## Buying Guide: What Professional Mechanics Need to Know

When choosing an industrial ultrasonic cleaner for carburetor restoration, keep these factors in mind:

* **Tank Size**: You must calculate the physical dimensions of the largest carburetor you work on. Don't just look at the liter capacity; ensure the length and width allow your parts to be fully submerged.
* **Heating Capabilities**: Never buy a cleaner without a heater. Heating the solution is essential for breaking down grease and varnish.
* **Frequency (kHz)**: Most industrial cleaners operate at 40kHz, which is the "sweet spot" for mechanical cleaning. It is powerful enough to remove carbon but gentle enough not to pit the aluminum.
* **Drainage Valve**: If you are getting a machine larger than 6 liters, a built-in drain valve is mandatory. Carrying a full 15-liter machine to a sink is a recipe for disaster.
