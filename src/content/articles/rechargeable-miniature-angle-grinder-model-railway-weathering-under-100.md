## Buyer's Guide: Choosing Your Perfect Miniature Angle Grinder

Selecting the right miniature angle grinder for your model railway weathering can significantly impact the quality and enjoyment of your hobby. Here's what to look for when making your choice under the $100 budget:

### 1. Power and Speed (RPM)

*   **Variable Speed Control:** This is perhaps the most crucial feature. Weathering often requires different speeds – higher RPMs for initial material removal or shaping, and lower RPMs for delicate sanding, buffing, or creating subtle textures. Look for a tool that offers a wide range of adjustable speeds.
*   **RPM Range:** For miniature work, a maximum RPM between 15,000 and 25,000 is generally sufficient. More important than the max speed is the ability to operate effectively at lower speeds without stalling.
*   **Voltage/Torque:** Higher voltage (e.g., 7.2V vs. 3.7V) generally translates to more torque, meaning the tool maintains its speed better under load. This is useful if you're dealing with slightly harder materials or more aggressive weathering.

### 2. Battery Life and Type

*   **Lithium-Ion (Li-Ion):** Most modern rechargeable tools use Li-Ion batteries due to their excellent power-to-weight ratio, lack of memory effect, and slow self-discharge.
*   **mAh Rating:** A higher milliamp-hour (mAh) rating indicates longer run time between charges. For extensive weathering sessions, look for 1500mAh or higher.
*   **Charging Method:** USB-C charging is incredibly convenient as it allows you to use common phone chargers or power banks. Micro-USB is also common. Consider charging time as well.

### 3. Ergonomics and Size

*   **Comfort:** You'll be holding this tool for extended periods, often with precise movements. A lightweight, slim, and well-balanced design with a comfortable grip is essential to prevent hand fatigue and ensure accuracy.
*   **Access to Tight Spaces:** Miniature models have miniature spaces. A compact head and overall small form factor are vital for reaching intricate areas without damaging surrounding details.

### 4. Attachment Compatibility and Accessories

*   **Shaft Size:** Most miniature rotary tools use a standard 2.3mm or 3.2mm (1/8 inch) collet. Ensure your chosen tool can accommodate a wide range of readily available accessories.
*   **Included Accessories:** Look for kits that include a variety of relevant attachments for weathering:
    *   **Grinding Stones:** For shaping, removing material, or creating deeper imperfections.
    *   **Sanding Bands/Discs:** Various grits for smoothing, texturing, or creating wear patterns.
    *   **Wire Brushes:** For texturing, creating rust effects, or cleaning.
    *   **Polishing Pads:** For subtle sheen or blending.
*   **Availability of Replacements:** Can you easily find replacement discs, sanding bands, and other consumables?

### 5. Durability and Build Quality

Even at a budget under $100, you want a tool that will last. Read reviews regarding the tool's construction, motor longevity, and the quality of its internal components. A well-built tool will withstand the vibrations and demands of regular use.

### 6. Price

Stick to your under $100 budget. Many excellent options exist in this price range, offering a great balance of features and performance for model railway weathering. Avoid going for the absolute cheapest option if it means sacrificing crucial features like variable speed or battery quality.
