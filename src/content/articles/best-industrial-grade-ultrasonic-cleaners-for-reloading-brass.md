## Buying Guide: What to Look For in 2025

When selecting an ultrasonic cleaner for brass, keep these three factors in mind:

1.  **Tank Capacity:** If you process large quantities of brass for long-range matches, opt for a unit with 6+ liters of capacity. A larger tank prevents "overcrowding," which is essential for effective cavitation.
2.  **Heating Elements:** Look for a cleaner with an integrated heater. Cold cleaning is inefficient for the heavy carbon fouling found on rifle brass; heat acts as a catalyst, cutting your cycle time down by 50% or more.
3.  **Drainage Systems:** You will be moving gallons of water and chemical solution. Units with built-in drainage ports are significantly safer and cleaner to operate than those you have to flip over to empty.
