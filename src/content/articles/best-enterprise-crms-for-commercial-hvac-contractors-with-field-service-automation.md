## Buying Guide: Choosing the Right CRM for HVAC

Selecting the right CRM is the most important technology investment you will make. Keep these four pillars in mind:

* **Field-to-Office Sync:** Does the technician have access to the equipment's entire service history in the field? Access to blueprints, previous repair notes, and manuals can save hours on a commercial job site.
* **Inventory Management:** For commercial HVAC, you aren't just selling labor; you're moving parts. Ensure your CRM tracks stock levels across different warehouse locations and van inventories.
* **Contract Automation:** Can the system automatically trigger service visits based on the specific parameters of your commercial maintenance contracts?
* **Scalability:** Don't buy a tool that fits your current size; buy one that fits the business you want to have in three years.
